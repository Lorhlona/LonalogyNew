\begin{abstract}
\noindent
String theory requires extra spatial dimensions for quantum-gravitational
consistency.  Despite extensive searches at the LHC
($M_D\gtrsim 5.5$--$11.2\,\mathrm{TeV}$ for ADD graviton emission,
$m_1\gtrsim 2.2$--$5.6\,\mathrm{TeV}$ for RS Kaluza--Klein gravitons)
and in short-distance gravity experiments ($R<44\,\mu\mathrm{m}$ for
$\delta=1$), no evidence for extra dimensions has been found.

We show that in the LoNalogy framework, literal extra spacetime
coordinates are unnecessary.  The core action is 4-dimensional;
the roles played by extra dimensions in string theory---gauge
unification, family structure, coupling hierarchy---are assumed by
the internal modular/fiber geometry of $X(2)$: the rank-5 bundle,
three-cusp family, and bridge/hidden sectors.  The integers
$\nu_{\br}=12$, $d_{\mathrm{fib}}=7$, $N=5$ that appear in
LoNalogy are internal bundle dimensions, not spacetime coordinates.

The conclusion is that extra dimensions are not denied but
\emph{de-literalised}: they are reinterpreted as internal
geometry of a 4D law, consistent with the mainstream AdS/CFT
reading of warped extra dimensions as 4D composite dynamics.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}
%% ============================================================

Since Kaluza (1921) and Klein (1926), the idea that spacetime may
have more than four dimensions has been a recurring theme in
theoretical physics.  String theory, in its most developed form,
requires 10 or 11 spacetime dimensions for anomaly cancellation and
quantum consistency.  The extra dimensions are typically assumed to
be compactified at a scale $R$, with Kaluza--Klein (KK) excitations
at masses $m_n\sim n/R$.

Three broad classes of extra-dimensional models have been studied
experimentally:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item \textbf{ADD} (Arkani-Hamed--Dimopoulos--Dvali): $\delta$
large flat extra dimensions with the fundamental scale $M_D$ at
the TeV scale.
\item \textbf{RS} (Randall--Sundrum): one warped extra dimension
with an exponential hierarchy between the Planck and TeV scales.
\item \textbf{UED} (Universal Extra Dimensions): all Standard Model
fields propagate in the bulk.
\end{enumerate}

Despite extensive searches, no evidence for any of these has been
found.  This paper examines the status of extra dimensions from
both the experimental side and the LoNalogy framework, and argues
that LoNalogy provides a 4D-complete alternative in which the
structural roles of extra dimensions are played by internal
modular/fiber geometry.

\subsection{Short-distance gravity}

Tabletop gravity experiments constrain large flat extra dimensions
directly.  For $\delta=1$ flat extra dimension, the current bound
is $R<44\,\mu\mathrm{m}$.  For $\delta=2$, ATLAS monojet data
give $R<3.8\,\mu\mathrm{m}$.

\subsection{Summary of experimental status}

strongly constrained toward exclusion.}}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\text{Planck-scale compactified extra dimensions:
logically possible but experimentally inaccessible.}}
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}

\subsection{Internal dimensions vs.\ spacetime dimensions}

Several large integers appear in the LoNalogy framework.  It is
essential to distinguish these from spacetime dimensions.

\begin{proposition}[Internal dimensions are not spacetime coordinates]\label{prop:internal}
The integers appearing in LoNalogy have the following origins:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item $\nu_{\br}=12$: the real dimension of the broken tangent space
$SU(5)/S(U(3)\times U(2))\cong\Gr_2(\C^5)$, computed as
$2\cdot 3\cdot 2=12$.  This is an \emph{internal coset dimension},
not a spacetime coordinate count.

\item $d_{\mathrm{fib}}=7$: the fiber/bridge dimension in the
three-sector decomposition.  This counts internal degrees of
freedom, not spatial directions.

\item $N=5$ (rank): selected by the Yukawa closure
$\Lambda^2 V\otimes\Lambda^2 V\otimes V\to\det V$ and anomaly
cancellation.  This is a \emph{bundle rank}, not a spacetime
dimension.
\end{enumerate}
None of these is an additional spacetime coordinate $x^\mu$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{remark}
The confusion between internal and spacetime dimensions is the
most common misreading of LoNalogy by those trained in string
theory, where internal dimensions \emph{are} literally extra
spacetime coordinates (compactified).
\end{remark}

\begin{proof}
Each row is verified by direct comparison with the LoNalogy
framework:

\emph{Gauge unification.}
String theory compactifies on a manifold $G/H$ whose isometry group
gives the gauge group.  LoNalogy obtains $SU(3)\times SU(2)\times U(1)$
from the rank-5 bundle on $X(2)$ via the anomaly-free decomposition
$5=3+2$.  No compact manifold with spatial coordinates is needed.

\emph{Family structure.}
In string theory, three generations arise from intersection numbers
or fixed points of the compact manifold.  In LoNalogy, three
generations correspond to the three cusps $\{0,1,\infty\}$ of $X(2)$.
These are modular-geometric features, not spatial locations.

\emph{Coupling hierarchy.}
The RS model generates the Planck/TeV hierarchy via the warp factor
$e^{-k r_c\pi}$.  LoNalogy generates the hierarchy via the bridge
reciprocity law $M_C\|\sigma_{\br}\|=\Lambda_*$ and the strict
kernel equation, which fixes the modular parameter algebraically.

\emph{KK tower.}
In KK theory, the tower $m_n=n/R$ arises from Fourier decomposition
on the compact direction.  In LoNalogy, the holonomy variable
$u\in[0,1)$ plays the analogous role (cf.\ the universal
projected-determinant theorem), but it is a \emph{fiber} coordinate,
not a spacetime coordinate.

\emph{Moduli stabilisation.}
String theory requires flux compactification to fix the moduli
(sizes and shapes of extra dimensions).  LoNalogy fixes its moduli
via the strict kernel equation
$\nu_{\br}(1-k)^2(1+k)=2$, which has a unique solution
$k_{\mathrm{ker}}$ by cubic analysis.

\emph{Hidden sector.}
In braneworld models, the hidden sector lives on a separate brane
separated in the extra dimension.  In LoNalogy, the hidden sector
$\fI$ is separated by the Schur complement structure, not by spatial
distance.
\end{proof}

The structural content of extra dimensions---unification, family
